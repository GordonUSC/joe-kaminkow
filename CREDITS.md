# Art, sources and credit

This is an independent tribute from Gordon Bellamy and his team. It is not affiliated with the companies or licensed entertainment properties discussed.

## Original and retained art

The showroom sculpture was modeled and rendered in Blender for this tribute. The car study, favicon and live reel symbols are original vector artwork. The sculpture is not a replica of a commercial game. The car study is not a photograph of Joe’s DB5. No editorial photography, branded game art or commercial game audio is reproduced in the new experience.

The existing pinball backglass, playfield and audio are preserved from this repository’s original tribute. Its original credits identify Higgsfield for the art and ElevenLabs for the audio. The new reel toy uses original synthesized tones, with sound off until requested.

Barlow, Barlow Condensed, Bungee, Bungee Shade and Silkscreen are self-hosted from the Google Fonts collection. Their SIL Open Font License files are included in `fonts/`.

## The historical record

- [Licensing International, 2024 Hall of Fame biography](https://licensinginternational.org/about/hall-of-fame/joseph-kaminkow/): the broad career arc and Joe’s licensing work.
- [Aristocrat, October 10, 2022 appointment announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aristocrat-gaming-appoints-joe-kaminkow-as-chief-innovation-officer-301644444.html): Chief Innovation Officer appointment and prior 2013 role. Company-reported contribution totals are not claims of sole authorship.
- [Joe’s 2023 interview with CDC Gaming](https://cdcgaming.com/commentary/frank-floor-talk-innovator-joe-kaminkow-looks-back-and-to-the-future/): video, sound, lighting, cabinet design and teamwork. Joe did not originate Buffalo, which predates his Aristocrat tenure.
- [Hagerty-attributed car interview, February 19, 2025](https://collectorscarworld.com/joe-kaminkows-james-bond-007-db5-heads-to-amelia/): the collection, DB5 project and collaborators. Build counts are attributed to that published account. The film-inspired cars are not presented as screen-used originals.
- [Pinball Expo 2025 program](https://pinballexpo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Expo-Guide-2025.pdf): Hall of Fame induction list, including Joe in 2004.
- [US Patent 5,358,244](https://patents.google.com/patent/US5358244A/en): Joseph E. Kaminkow and Edwin Cebula, a movable ball-retrieval target assembly.
- [Laser War record](https://www.ipdb.org/machine.cgi?id=1415) and [Pinside’s team record](https://pinside.com/pinball/machine/laser-war/details): Joe’s design, David Thiel’s music/sound, and the account of making the game with Gary Stern.
- [Barry Oursler’s catalog](https://pinside.com/pinball/machine/barry-oursler): Space Shuttle design credit; Joe’s NASA licensing contribution is separately documented by Licensing International.

All fourteen original career moments retain their reference links on `pinball.html`. Hall of Fame inductions are separated from other industry recognition. The personal dedication expresses Gordon’s admiration for Joe’s creativity and generosity.

The new Imagination Machine is free creative play: no money, purchases, prizes, cash value or gambling services. All nine stories are available without playing.
