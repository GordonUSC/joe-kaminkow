The Arc

A career told in five chapters, no easy ones.

1960s · Maryland

Born into the trade

Father was a Bally VP / General Vending exec. Joe grew up around the cabinets — the trade wasn't a career choice so much as a birthright.

1983 · Curry College

Diploma, then the floor

Curry College, '83. Then straight into the industry that raised him.

1984 · Williams Electronics

Space Shuttle saves Williams

His first signature pinball release. Industry lore credits the title with keeping the lights on at Williams during a brutal stretch for coin-op. Joe was barely out of school.

Nov 1986 · Data East Pinball

Co-founds Data East with Gary Stern

EVP Game Design. The Data East / Sega Pinball years produce a string of licensed-IP classics — Last Action Hero, Tales from the Crypt, Jurassic Park, Apollo 13, Space Jam, Star Wars Trilogy: Special Edition, South Park.

1999–~2010 · IGT Reno

Invents the branded slot

Builds the Wheel of Fortune slot franchise — the title that effectively created the branded-IP slot category. Sex and the City, The Price Is Right, Ghostbusters follow. IGT names him Inventor of the Year.

2010–2013 · The pause

Reality TV. Broadway. Baseball.

A three-year off-ramp by design. Minority owner of the Harrisburg Senators (Double-A). Producing credits in unscripted TV. Time on Broadway. The kind of pause most people in this business never give themselves.

2013 · Spooky Cool → Zynga

Co-founds Spooky Cool Labs. Sells to Zynga (Jun 19, 2013).

Becomes Chief Creative Officer / Chief Game Designer at Zynga. Ships Hit It Rich!, Wizard of Oz, Willy Wonka, Harry Potter on the social-casino layer.

Oct 10, 2022 · Aristocrat

Chief Innovation Officer, Aristocrat Gaming

Scope: Aristocrat Gaming + Pixel United + Anaxi (online RMG). Reports to CPO Matt Primmer. The NFL slot line — Super Bowl Jackpots, Overtime Cash, NFL Kickoff, Winning Drive, Rings of Victory — happens here.

2014–present · Ka-Pow Pinball

The personal studio

Runs Ka-Pow Pinball as a creative side project, collaborating with Stern. Releases Batman '66 (2016) and The Beatles (2018, 1,964-unit limited). The pinball vein never closes.
